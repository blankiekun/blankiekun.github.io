Privacy

This is the privacy policy for blankiekun.github.io, including CONscripter's Google Drive sync. Last updated October 15, 2026.

Games and saves

CONscripter plays games from your own computer. Game files are read straight from your disk and are never uploaded. Saves are kept in your browser's storage on your device. If you turn on File > Settings > Keep a copy of games, the game files are copied into that storage too, and still never leave your device.

Google Drive sync

If you turn on File > Sync with Google Drive in CONscripter, you sign in with Google and allow the site to use the drive.appdata permission. With it:

  • The site keeps one file per game in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, holding that game's save files. It can't see or change anything else in your Drive.
  • Your browser talks to Google directly. Saves and the sign-in token never pass through any server of this site. The token is kept only for the browser tab's session.
  • The site reads your Google account's email address once, to show which account sync is using. It's stored only in your browser.
  • Nothing from your Google account is shared with anyone, used for ads, or used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To stop syncing, choose File > Turn Off Google Drive Sync. To delete a game's copies, use Debug > Delete This Game's Saves while sync is on. To delete all of them, open Google Drive, then Settings > Manage Apps, find this site and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the site's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Sending drawings and notes

If you use Send in Paint or Notepad, what you send goes to the site owner through a Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks you aren't a bot.
