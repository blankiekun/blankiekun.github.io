Welcome to blankiekun

Read Japanese. Play visual novels. In the browser.

Three open-source projects that fit together. chimimouryou is a self-hosted reader for your Calibre library. CONscripter is the visual novel engine it uses to play games in the browser. conscripter-convert turns games made with other engines into games CONscripter can play.

How a game reaches your browser
Your gamea visual novel you own, e.g. 最果てのイマ
conscripter-convertXuse archives and scripts → 0.txt + arc.nsa

chimimouryoulays the text over the game for Yomitan
Did you know...

Every page CONscripter draws in chimimouryou hands its text, and where each character landed, to the web page. That is what lets you look words up with Yomitan and count what you read in your reading statistics.

chimimouryou

ちみもうりょう

A self-hosted Japanese e-book reader for your Calibre library. It is a fork of ッツ Ebook Reader that runs on your own server, so everyone in the house reads from one library with their own account, synced progress and highlights, plus tools for language learners.

What it adds
  • Your Calibre library as the bookshelf: covers, series, tags, "Continue reading", filters and sorting.
  • Accounts with an admin panel. Progress, statistics, highlights and settings sync across devices.
  • Highlights, notes and full-text search that ignores furigana.
  • AI helpers with your own keys: Ask AI, spoiler-free "Story so far" recaps, a lorebook, and read-aloud with the current line highlighted.
  • Manga (CBZ/ZIP) in a page-turning reader.
  • Visual novels played in the browser with CONscripter, with saves synced and the text counted in your stats.
  • Difficulty estimates from Jiten's features, computed on your server.

conscripter-convert

conscripter-convert

Converts Japanese visual novels you own into games that run on CONscripter, including its WebAssembly build. It reads a game's archives, images, audio and compiled scripts, translates them into an engine-neutral intermediate form, and writes a 0.txt script plus .nsa archives. It ships no game data and reads only your own installation.

C:\> conscripter-convert convert-game D:\SAIHATE -o out\imaa --font ipag.ttf --title "最果てのイマ"

Decompiling scripts ..... ok
Writing 0.txt, arc.nsa .. ok

How it works
  • Each source engine is a separate reader that feeds the same output stage. Xuse is the first; Nitroplus is in progress.
  • Anything it can't convert yet stays in 0.txt as a comment naming the source instruction, and each run prints a ranked list of what was left out.
  • When a game needs something NScripter can't do, the feature is added to CONscripter rather than approximated.

CONscripter

An ONScripter engine for playing NScripter visual novels on the web, Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and RetroArch. It is a fork of ONScripter-Yuri, built on SDL2, and the engine behind chimimouryou's visual novel player and conscripter-convert's output.

Commands it adds to NScripter 
; night tint on sprite 10
spfilter 10,1,1,256,148,152,200
; black silhouette
spfilter 10,1,0,256,0,0,0
; run a scene, then put the screen back
pushscreen 0,999:gosub *scene:popscreen:print 10

Every addition is opt-in. Scripts that don't use them behave exactly as in ONScripter-Yuri, and the extra state is kept in save files.
