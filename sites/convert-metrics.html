How much of each game converts

A game's scripts are a long list of commands: show this picture, play this sound, say this line. The converter rewrites each one for CONscripter. The bar shows how many made it across; a missing command is one the converted game skips or does only roughly.

Fine print

Commands in parts of a game no player can reach, such as a debug menu, are skipped on purpose and count as converted. A command that converts with some of its settings ignored counts partly: one with one of three settings ignored counts two thirds. The numbers come from the converter's own test run and are read live from its main branch, so they can lag a change by up to 5 minutes. Under the date is the version of the converter they were measured on.
