Hi, and thanks for stopping by! I make three open-source projects that fit together. chimimouryou is a self-hosted reader for your Calibre library. CONscripter is the visual novel engine it uses to play games in the browser. conscripter-convert turns games made with other engines into games CONscripter can play.

My Projects

CONscripterC++ · GPL-2.0Plays NScripter games on the web and everywhere else.

How a game reaches your browser

Your gamea visual novel you own, e.g. 最果てのイマ → conscripter-convertXuse archives and scripts → 0.txt + arc.nsa → CONscripterplays it natively or as WebAssembly → chimimouryoulays the text over the game for Yomitan
Did you know... Every page CONscripter draws in chimimouryou hands its text, and where each character landed, to the web page. That is what lets you look words up with Yomitan and count what you read in your reading statistics.
