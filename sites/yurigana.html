yuriganaWhich reading does this kanji word take, here?

The same two kanji, 人気, read にんき (popularity) in the first sentence and ひとけ (people around) in the second. Furigana helpers that look a word up on its own get one of them wrong. yurigana reads each kanji word from the sentence around it, and keeps the furigana a book prints. It gives chimimouryou its readings and its "Known" furigana, which hides the ones you no longer need.
