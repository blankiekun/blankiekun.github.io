人気にんきの店
a popular shop
人気ひとけのない道
a deserted road

The same two kanji, read two ways. Furigana helpers that look a word up on its own get one of them wrong. yurigana reads each kanji word from the sentence around it, and keeps the furigana a book prints. It gives chimimouryou its readings and its "Known" furigana, which hides the ones you no longer need. Get it on GitHub.
